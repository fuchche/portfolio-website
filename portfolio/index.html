Integrating AI Tools in Computer Science Classrooms

Published by Yubaraj Shrestha on August 2026

Artificial Intelligence is revolutionizing secondary computer science education. Rather than replacing fundamental logic building, generative AI models act as dynamic personal tutors for students learning languages like Python and C.

In my classes, we use AI tools for syntax debugging exercises, generating test cases, and exploring algorithmic efficiency comparisons. This approach boosts student confidence and encourages deeper problem solving.
